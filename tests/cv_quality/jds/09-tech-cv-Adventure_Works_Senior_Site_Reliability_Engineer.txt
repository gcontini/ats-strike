⚡ The Day-to-Day


Master the Domain: Partner with engineering teams to set crystal-clear reliability objectives (SLIs/SLOs) and build long-term reliability roadmaps for core services.
Tackle High-Stakes Incidents: Lead cross-service incident responses during critical outages, guiding teams with confidence and translating chaos into clear, durable solutions.
Architect Resilient Futures: Drive the company-wide adoption of cutting-edge reliability patterns, deployment safety capabilities, and infrastructure scalability.
Elevate Observability: Build and refine advanced observability tools to catch issues before they reach production.
Champion Engineering Balance: Influence product design and system architecture to maintain the ultimate equilibrium between feature velocity and rock-solid reliability.
Empower the Team: Mentor senior engineers, elevate best practices, and play a pivotal role in recruiting top-tier talent to the SRE organization.


🧠 Your Superpowers


Battle-Tested Expertise: Bring 6–10 years of SRE, DevOps, or production engineering experience with hands-on mastery of complex distributed systems.
Cloud Architecture Wizardry: Command cloud infrastructure (AWS preferred), container orchestration, and Infrastructure as Code to keep modern platforms humming.
Engineering Precision: Craft clean, high-performing code in at least one major language alongside a knack for automating away repetitive tasks.
Incident Command Ready: Show a proven track record of steering large-scale incident management and driving high-impact reliability programs.
Cross-Functional Influence: Communicate effortlessly across engineering, product, and customer-facing teams to align tech goals with business impact.

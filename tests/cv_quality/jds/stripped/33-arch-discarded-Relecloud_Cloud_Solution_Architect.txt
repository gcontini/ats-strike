Cloud Solution Architect

MISSION

The Solution Architect team at Relecloud supports the Sales function by structuring value-driven solutions for our customers and prospects, as well as responding to RFPs, RFQs, and tenders. On a daily basis, requirements must be analyzed and understood to structure the best solution to propose. The team is responsible for defining and drafting technical offers, supported by Operations teams, determining the effort required from involved departments and the costs of proposed solutions.

RESPONSIBILITIES AND ACTIVITIES

Analyze customer business objectives, technical requirements and pain points
Collaborate with cross-functional internal teams on strategic projects
Supporting the design of technical solutions tailored to customer requirements according to Relecloud's methodology and service positioning
Supporting Sales and Channel Partner teams throughout all phases of the sales process and customer management
Proactively proposing new solutions consistent with the evolution of Cloud services to be included in Relecloud's service portfolio
Collaborating with Project, Service Delivery, and Operations teams during service implementation phases, as well as gathering requirements for upselling opportunities with existing customers
Drafting technical offer documents and presentations for Relecloud services

EXPERIENCE AND EDUCATIONAL QUALIFICATION

At least 2 years of experience in pre-sales or similar roles
High School Diploma or Scientific Degree

SKILLS AND KNOWLEDGE

Knowledge of VMware and KVM virtualization architectures and products
Knowledge of IBM Power ecosystem architectures (IBM i, AIX)
Knowledge of Windows and Linux server operating systems
Knowledge of major IaaS and PaaS services on Public Cloud platforms (Azure, AWS, Google Cloud)
Understanding of Disaster Recovery processes and Business Continuity mechanisms (RPO, RTO, SLA, etc.)
Knowledge of industry-standard strategies and tools for server and system migrations.
Knowledge in preparing and presenting economic P&L related to an offer
Professional proficiency in English, both written and spoken
Strong proficiency in Microsoft Office suite (Excel, Word, PowerPoint)
Preferred: Skills in Cybersecurity Managed Services
Preferred: Knowledge of AI platforms
Preferred: Recognized industry certifications in Cloud Architecture, Enterprise Solutions or Security Management.

ATTITUDES AND SKILLS

Capability to assimilate and process information from various sources
Rapid learning ability and curiosity for emerging technologies
Goal-oriented mindset with strong focus on deliverables and deadlines
Collaborative approach in working with multiple internal stakeholders
Excellent communication skills, with a talent for explaining complex technical topics to business buyers.
Strategic thinking to understand business applications of new technologies
Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities with creative approach to solution design
Business acumen to understand and articulate revenue and cost implications

AI Project Manager

Are you a Project Manager expert in getting specified use cases delivered — on scope, on date, with no late surprises?

WHAT YOU WILL FIND

Core activities. Once a use case is specified, you make sure it gets delivered — on scope, on date, with the client knowing where it stands throughout. You will hold the client interface from the specification sprint to the final handover: domain-expert workshops, C-level steering meetings, status and bad news early. You will plan the delivery phases — data, adaptation, eval, deployment — and close each one on an explicit exit criterion. And you will keep allocation, effort and timeline against plan, putting every scope change through formal change control.
Tech & tools stack.. Delivery may be a custom model built end to end, a distilled or optimised version of an existing one, an agentic system tuned for cost and latency, a platform rollout on the client's side, or a broader AI programme spanning several of these. You will work with project and portfolio tooling and read — not write — what the engineering team produces: eval reports, GPU-hour and token estimates, and the traceability of runs, datasets and models.
Mentoring, collaboration, and continuous growth. You are the single counterpart for the client and the connective tissue inside: model design engineers upstream, research and platform engineers building, security and legal on the data dependencies (DPAs, licensing, anonymisation, client samples). Clients are large enterprises and public institutions, typically with a C-level sponsor and a technical team of their own.

WHAT WE NEED

Academic background. Degree in Engineering Management Engineering, Economics or a related field. A technical or quantitative background will help you hold your own with the engineering team.
Technical & strategic skills. 3-8 years of professional experience. A proven delivery track record in MLOps, AI products and/or Generative models, with clients as your direct counterpart rather than through an account layer, and real planning, budgeting and change-control discipline on projects with hard external dependencies. You don't need to train models or build agents, but you do need to read an eval report, sanity-check a GPU-hour or token estimate, and know why a failed run costs both budget and calendar. You can plan around a scarce shared resource — queued compute, a benchmark window, a client data handover — where the constraint sets the schedule, not headcount. The position is open to varying levels of expertise and seniority.
Soft skills. Strong facilitation, synthesis and executive communication, ownership without needing formal authority, and fluent English.
Nice to have. PMP, PRINCE2 or Agile certification; delivery experience on AI/ML, MLOps or data-platform programmes; regulated or public-sector programmes; pre-sales and proposal work; multi-vendor or EU-funded programme management.

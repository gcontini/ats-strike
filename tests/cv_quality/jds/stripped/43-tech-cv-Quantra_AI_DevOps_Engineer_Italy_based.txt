DevOps Engineer (Italy based)

Role Overview

Quantra AI, a leading provider of AI-driven investment solutions, is looking for an Intermediate DevOps Engineer to join our Platform Team. You will play a key role in supporting, enhancing, and maintaining the foundational infrastructure and tools that empower our product teams — contributing to a reliable, scalable, and efficient developer platform that enables fast and secure software delivery for Sphere, our proprietary AI-driven investment platform.

Key Responsibilities

Contribute to the design, implementation, and automation of CI/CD pipelines (GitLab CI/CD) to ensure fast, safe, and repeatable releases across all environments.
Assist in the adoption and management of Infrastructure as Code using Terraform and Ansible, helping provision and manage on-premise and AWS resources in a controlled, auditable, and reproducible manner.
Work with Docker and Kubernetes to package and operate services, contributing to declarative configuration, GitOps practices, and efficient resource utilization.
Help implement and maintain monitoring, logging, and tracing solutions (Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, Zabbix), and participate in on-call rotations to ensure high availability and low Mean Time To Recovery (MTTR).
Contribute to embedding security best practices (OWASP Top 10, least privilege IAM, shift-left testing) into the SDLC and help automate security controls within pipelines.
Support the management and automation of database infrastructure (MySQL, MongoDB), including provisioning, backups, and automated schema migrations.
Automate and support the deployment, scaling, and operational health of core platform services (authentication, data pipelines, messaging) for internal consumers.
Participate in technical design discussions, learn from architectural decisions related to infrastructure and operations, and help document runbooks and standards.
Champion DevOps culture ("you build it, you run it") and share knowledge through demos, pair programming, and internal workshops.

Skills Required

Proven experience in a DevOps or Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) role, with a focus on automation, infrastructure, and CI/CD
Hands-on experience working with and contributing to CI/CD pipelines
Practical experience with Docker and exposure to Kubernetes, including writing Dockerfiles and debugging containerized applications
Familiarity with Infrastructure as Code tools such as Terraform or Ansible
Experience with at least one monitoring, logging, or alerting system (e.g., Prometheus, Grafana, ELK Stack, Zabbix), and curiosity for SRE principles
Proficiency in at least one scripting language (e.g., Python, Bash) for automating common tasks
Basic understanding of security best practices in the SDLC
Good analytical skills and a methodical approach to troubleshooting system and infrastructure issues
Clear English communication, collaborative spirit, and a growth mindset aligned with a culture of shared ownership and rapid feedback

Bonus points:

Experience with event-driven systems (e.g., ActiveMQ)
Contributions to open-source projects
Familiarity with backend development concepts (e.g., Java/Spring Boot)
Working knowledge of AWS fundamentals (EC2, S3, IAM)
Financial-services or high-throughput data processing background

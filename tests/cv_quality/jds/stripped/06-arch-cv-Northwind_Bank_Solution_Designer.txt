Solution Designer

The Solution Designer / IT Integrator is responsible for translating business objectives, functional requirements and non-functional requirements into scalable, secure and maintainable end-to-end technical solutions.

The role acts as the bridge between Business, Architecture and Engineering, ensuring technical coherence across systems, applications, integrations and data flows while supporting successful delivery outcomes.

The Solution Designer / IT Integrator drives solution feasibility, end-to-end design, technical alignment and implementation governance throughout the initiative lifecycle.

Key Responsibilities

Business & Solution Analysis

    Analyse business requirements, Epics and User Stories.
    Support shaping and feasibility activities.
    Perform impact assessments across applications, services and business processes.
    Ensure traceability between requirements and technical implementation.

Solution Design

    Design end-to-end technical solutions aligned with enterprise architecture principles.
    Define integration patterns, APIs, events, data flows and system interactions.
    Produce solution documentation and technical specifications.
    Ensure compliance with security, resilience and operational requirements.

Delivery Support

    Support backlog refinement and User Story decomposition.
    Collaborate with development teams during implementation.
    Drive technical decision-making and design reviews.
    Support testing, deployment and production readiness activities.

Stakeholder Management

    Collaborate with Product Owners, Architects, Engineers and Business stakeholders.
    Manage technical dependencies across squads and domains.
    Facilitate workshops and solution alignment sessions.
    Communicate risks, assumptions and constraints.

Quality & Governance

    Promote engineering excellence and best practices.
    Ensure alignment with architecture, security and compliance standards.
    Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives.
    Support operational stability and problem resolution activities.

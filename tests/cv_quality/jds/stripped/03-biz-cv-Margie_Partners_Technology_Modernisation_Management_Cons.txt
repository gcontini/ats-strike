Technology Modernisation - Management Consultant

What You’ll Do

Our Technology Modernization team provides independent expertise that enables executives to unlock value from technology and enhance business performance. We deliver rapid and sustainable impact across transformation initiatives, with a strong focus on modernization and strategic enablement. Key areas include:

Defining technology strategy and roadmaps aligned with business goals and value creation
Rapid diagnostics and deep-dive assessments to identify performance gaps and root causes
Platform modernization, including legacy tech-debt assessment, re-platforming strategies, and program recovery
Business process modernization leveraging innovative technologies (e.g., automation, AI agents)
Operating model design and transformation to enable scalable, product-oriented delivery
Technology cost optimization and transparency through benchmarking and spend analysis
Technology due diligence, carve-out, and post-merger integration planning and execution
Complex program assurance and turnaround for high-stakes digital transformations

Practical IT knowledge in one or more areas: software delivery, IT infrastructure and operations, solutions architecture, IT governance (audit, risk, compliance, security), and/or IT project management
Experience implementing projects in diverse environments using various methodologies and frameworks
Strong personal interest in technology and digital trends, staying current on industry developments
Passion for driving IT effectiveness and enabling clients to achieve business objectives
Confidence in collaborating with clients and stakeholders, building relationships, influencing, and advising decision-makers
Excellent communication and presentation skills, including the ability to engage at CXO and senior levels
Fluency in English and Italian (written and spoken)
Outstanding analytical and critical thinking skills, combined with strong financial literacy
Knowledge or experience in M&A contexts is an advantage
Ability and willingness to travel across EMEA and work outside standard business hours when required
Comfortable working in a hybrid model (office + remote) and dynamic environments

Senior Enterprise IT Transformation Architect

The Role

We are looking for an architect that can manage complex infrastructure and application transformations of Enterprise level customers. The ideal candidate has significant experience in complex Cloud migration and adoption, integrated hybrid architectures, design and deployment of cloud-native architectures.

They should be adept at leading and driving transition and transformation projects for infrastructure services and Application Modernizations supporting the Digital Transformation of our clients and transforming to a new modern operating model. They have experience transforming Enterprise Outsourcing Infrastructure Services on distributed, mainframe, and hybrid cloud environments.

Key responsibilities will include:

    Manage complex infrastructure and application transformations for enterprise-level customers.
    Lead strategic planning and business analysis for cloud migration, adoption, and modernization initiatives.
    Design and deploy integrated hybrid architectures and cloud-native solutions that align with business goals.
    Drive transition and transformation (T&T) projects for infrastructure services across diverse environments.
    Transform Enterprise Outsourcing Infrastructure Services, including distributed systems, mainframes, and hybrid cloud platforms.
    Develop and evaluate enterprise architecture designs, ensuring alignment with technology governance and best practices.
    Engage and manage stakeholders, including technical teams and business leaders, to ensure architectural alignment and adoption.
    Assess risks and oversee implementation, ensuring compliance

As an Enterprise Architect with Globex, you will have the chance to work with some of the world's most innovative and dynamic companies, helping them to achieve their business goals through the strategic implementation of technology, including AI and Cloud adoption, Cloud Optimization, Cloud engineering, Data platforms.

You will need to be able to understand our customers’ needs and constraints and apply industry knowledge and best practices in areas such as Platform Engineering, Modern Operation, and Hybrid IT Optimization and Management, to develop effective IT solutions.

In addition to your technical skills, you will also need to be a skilled communicator, able to influence customers and sell our services using your in-depth knowledge of leading-edge technology solutions and your understanding of their business and industry.

If you're passionate about connecting an organization's business mission to its IT strategy, designing solutions that are effective, agile, sustainable, and flexible, and enjoy working with a talented team of IT professionals – join us at Globex as an Enterprise Architect and help build a better digital future for our customers.

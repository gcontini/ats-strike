You write two lines of one work experience of a candidate's CV: its ROLE SUMMARY and its COMPACT LINE. They are
written once and reused for every job application: code prints your text as it is. The bullets of this job are
written after you, by a writer who is shown your role summary and never repeats it.

INPUT
WRITING STYLE: how the candidate writes.
EXPERIENCE: title, company and dates of the job. The CV prints them in the job's header, above your lines.
EXPERIENCE CONTEXT: the items that describe the whole job: the system, the client, the team, the mission. The role
summary states every one of them.
ITEMS: every base item of the job: id, type (context, action or result), agency (led, did or supported), text, and
when present the scope (a limit on the candidate's role), the technologies, and the weight of the duty it comes from
(3 = the main work of the job, 2 = a substantial or recurring part, 1 = occasional or minor).

THE ITEMS ARE THE ONLY SOURCE: state nothing they do not state. No number, technology, client, scale or outcome that
is not in an item. "Led", "managed", "headed" only where an item has agency "led".

ROLE SUMMARY
Printed under the job's header, before its bullets. It introduces the job: the system, the client, the team, the
mission: what a reader needs in order to understand every bullet that follows.
* It states every item of EXPERIENCE CONTEXT, compressed to what a reader needs: you may shorten an item, never
  drop it.
* It tells no action and no result: they are the bullets' job.
* It does not repeat the header: the title, the company and the dates are printed just above it.
* At most 180 characters, in one or two sentences. Count the characters, spaces included.
* uses: the ids of every item it takes a fact from: every item of EXPERIENCE CONTEXT, and context items only.

COMPACT LINE
A job may be printed with its header and this ONE line only, to keep the career complete when the job matters little
for the application.
* At most 95 characters, starting with a past-tense verb.
* It tells the main work of the job, the weight-3 duties first, with the technologies or methods a recruiter searches
  for. Dense, but a sentence, not a list of keywords.
* uses: the ids of the items it draws on, at least one of them an action.

OUTPUT
One JSON object and nothing else, compact, without indentation:
{"role_summary": {"text": "...", "uses": ["e0.i01", "e0.i02"]}, "compact": {"text": "...", "uses": ["e0.i05", "e0.i09"]}}

WRITING RULES
The rules below hold for everything you write. AGENCY and SENTENCE VARIETY are about bullets and the compact line: the
role summary describes the job and tells no action.

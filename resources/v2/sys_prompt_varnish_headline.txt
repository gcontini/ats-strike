You write two pieces of the top of a CV made for one job: the JOB TITLE printed under the candidate's name, and
one SENTENCE of the summary.

INPUT
JD TITLE and TOP REQUIREMENTS of the job description (name, importance, terms).
TITLES HELD: the titles of the candidate's experiences. TITLES ALLOWED: titles the candidate may use.
FIXED TITLE: when present, the job title is this one; write only the sentence.
SUMMARY: the summary as it will be printed, with <<job_title>> where the job title goes and <<sentence>> where
your sentence goes.
EXPERIENCES: the CV's experiences as printed: the facts you may draw on.

JOB TITLE
At most 60 characters. It tells a recruiter the candidate is the person this job asks for: take the JD's title
and adjust it to what the candidate held or may use. Never a rank the candidate did not hold ("Head",
"Director", "Manager", "Lead") unless a title held or allowed has it.

SENTENCE
One sentence, at most {max_chars} characters, that fits where <<sentence>> stands and ties the candidate to
what the job asks most. Only facts the summary or the experiences state; no number that is not in them.
It must not repeat what the rest of the summary already says.

OUTPUT
One JSON object and nothing else: {"job_title": "...", "sentence": "..."}

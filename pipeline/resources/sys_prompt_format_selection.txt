Input:
* KEY POINTS: JSON list of the job's requirements. Each has id, requirement, keywords, importance (0-100). Item 1 is the job title.
* FORMATS: JSON object of the CV formats: for each one, how many work experiences it shows, the minimum and
  maximum duties of each experience, and the maximum duties overall. Every format fits the page: they trade depth for breadth.
* WORK EXPERIENCES: JSON list from the candidate's profile, most recent first. Each duty ends with "|<scores>",
  e.g. "|3-80,7-40": the KEY POINTS it matches (by id), each with how well it matches (0-100).

Choose the CV format, the work experiences to show and how many duties each one shows.
* The most recent work experience and the longest one is always shown.
* Judge how relevant each experience is on three signals together:
  - Duties: how high its 2-3 best duties score on important KEY POINTS.
  - Role: how well its title and role summary match the job title and the most important KEY POINTS, read as a recruiter would:
    the title says what the candidate did every day, the duties only tell a few stories of it.
  - Years: how long it lasted. A recruiter counts the years spent on the job's core skills: an experience that holds most of
    those years is relevant even when its duties score lower, because without it the CV cannot show them.
  Age alone does not make an experience less relevant.
* Choose the format by what one more experience adds: take a format with more experiences when the next experience proves an important
  KEY POINT (by role, years or duties) better than the experiences already chosen, even if they lose their lowest-scoring duties.
  Otherwise prefer fewer experiences with more duties.
* duty_caps: how many duties each chosen experience shows, within the format's minimum and maximum and its maximum
  overall, never more than the work experience has. Give more duty_caps to the more relevant experiences.
  - experiences whose duties score higher get more

Output only JSON, with the experience ids in profile order and one duty cap for each, in the same order:
{"reason": "<why this format and these experiences, one or two sentences>", "format": "B", "experience_ids": [0, 1, 2, 4], "duty_caps": [5, 6, 3, 2]}
